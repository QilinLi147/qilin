Qilin Li (**Member**, **IEEE**) received the B.Eng. degree in electrical engineering and automation
from the Wuhan University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, Hubei, China, in 2018, 
and the M.S. degree in computer science from the University of Wollongong, 
Wollongong, NSW, Australia, in 2020. He received the Ph.D. degree in computer science and technology
from South China University of Technology, Guangzhou, China, in 2026.

He is currently a postdoctoral researcher with the Department of Automation,
Tsinghua University, Beijing, China.

His research interests include **affective computing**, **neural networks**,
**EEG-based emotion recognition**, **music emotion recognition**, and **multimodal learning**.
