Our paper *"Memo2496: Expert-Annotated Dataset and Dual-View Adaptive Framework for Music Emotion Recognition"* has been accepted for publication in **IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing**.

The work introduces Memo2496, a dataset of 2,496 instrumental tracks with continuous valence-arousal annotations from 30 certified music specialists, together with DAMER, a dual-view adaptive framework for robust music emotion recognition.

- **Paper**: [10.1109/TAFFC.2026.3715195](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAFFC.2026.3715195)
- **Preprint**: [arXiv:2512.13998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13998)
- **Code**: [QilinLi147/DAMER](https://github.com/QilinLi147/DAMER)
